# QuarkOS Capes 1.0.0

First public release for Fabric and Forge on Minecraft 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.8, and 1.21.11, and for NeoForge on 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.8, and 1.21.11.

- Cape styles: flat, animated frame strip, cloth wave, 3D model cloak. Bundled samples: Bands, Slide, Cloak.
- Physics: Classic or Cloth (soft/stiff), Smooth or Blocky, wind, gravity.
- Settings screen with six categories (My Cape, Style, Physics, Sources, Gallery, Advanced) and a live preview. Browse is its own screen.
- Browse capes and cloaks from the QuarkOS Capes site in game, preview, Wear and Unwear.
- Online players with Show others enabled see website-worn and published capes. LabyMod wearing references also require that source switch. Local samples and edits stay local until shared.
- Link a website account with a code.
- Publish, search, and unpublish your own cape in the gallery.
- Optional read-only sources: OptiFine, LabyMod, MinecraftCapes, Cosmetica, Cloaks+.
- Offline mode on by default.
