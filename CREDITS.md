## LabyMod

The Browse → LabyMod gallery loads LabyMod cloaks live from laby.net (`laby.net/api/v3/search/textures/cloak`, `texture.laby.net`). No LabyMod texture is bundled in the jar or stored by the gallery; each cloak shows its laby.net page, and cloaks are try-on only. LabyMod and laby.net belong to LabyMedia GmbH. Quark's Capes is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by LabyMedia GmbH or LabyMod. Cloak artwork belongs to its uploaders.

## Minecraft

Not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. The mod offers no catalog of official Minecraft capes; a player's own Minecraft cape is drawn by the game as usual.
